Deep Reinforcement Learning: DQN & Rainbow

Replace linear FA with a deep network $Q(s,a;\theta)$ and stabilize learning with two tricks that made Atari possible: experience replay and a target network.

Why naive deep Q fails

  • Correlated consecutive samples break SGD assumptions.
  • Bootstrapped targets move as θ moves → oscillation / divergence (deadly triad).

DQN update

y = R + γ maxa' Q(S', a'; θ⁻)   (θ⁻ = frozen target weights)
Loss = ( Q(S,A; θ) − y )²

Store transitions $(s,a,r,s',done)$ in a replay buffer; sample minibatches; periodically copy $\theta \leftarrow \theta^-$.

Double DQN reduces overestimation: select $a^* = \arg\max_{a'} Q(s',a';\theta)$ but evaluate with $Q(s', a^*; \theta^-)$.

DQN engineering checklist

  • Frame stack (4) + max-pool over 2 frames for Atari.

  • ε-greedy with linear decay; eval with ε=0.01.
  • Log return, Q-mean, buffer size, and seed.
